# Business Insights

## Summary
This analysis only reviews clean and complete sales data and separate rows that need to be reviewed before being used in business decision making.

| Scope | Business Question | Business Insights | Recommended Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Category Performance | Which category generates the highest profit? What about the profit margin? | Electronics leads in total revenue (Rp681.51 Jt) and profit (Rp187.27 Jt). But it scored the lowest profit margin (27.48%). Meanwhile, Stationery generates the highest profit margin (46.85%) despite ranking 4th in total profit. | Check Electronics pricing and production cost, and determine whether the given discounts are too aggressive. Then, promote categories with high profit margin such as Stationery and Office Supplies through cross-selling to increase profitability. |
| Regional Performance | Which region generates the highest profit? What about the profit margin? | Makassar generates the highest profit (Rp87.32 Jt) but operates at a lower margin 29.19%. Meanwhile, Medan has the highest profit margin (30.79%) despite having lowest profit (Rp71.86 Jt). | Investigate why Makassar has lower profit margin despite high sales volume by reviewing its product mix and average discount rate. And also, examine the cost-efficiency factors in Medan and Bandung to replicate them in high-volume regions.|
| Channel Performance | Which channel generates the highest profit? What about the profit margin? | Corporate generates the highest profit (Rp103.36 Jt) but scored the second-lowest profit margin (28.92%). Meanwhile, Retail has the highest profit margin (31.12%) while ranking second in total profit (Rp102.50 Jt). | Investigate the causes of low margins in Corporate and Marketplace (e.g., platform fees, bulk discounting, or product mix). And also, apply successful strategies from Retail to Online channels, which currently has lowest revenue and profit.| 
| Monthly Trend Analysis | Are there months with unusually high or low performance? | March peaked in both revenue (Rp265.28 Jt) and profit (Rp79.77 Jt). On the other hand, February hit the lowest revenue (Rp163.14 Jt) and profit (Rp47.18 Jt) due to a -32.08% drop in orders. Despite fluctuations in total order and revenue, monthly profit margins remained stable about 28-30%. | Investigate the February drop (e.g., fewer calendar days, stock shortages, or seasonal factors) to mitigate future downturns. Then, analyze marketing drivers behind March spike and apply similar promotional campaigns during slow months (e.g., April and May). Since profit margin are stable, the key to increase total profit might be increasing the total order. |
| Sales Performance | Who is the sales rep with best performance based on highest profit and revenue? What about based on highest total order? | Raka is the top revenue contributor (Rp196.24 Jt) with the highest AOV (Rp3.22 Jt). Although, he has the fewest orders (61). Meanwhile, Sari leads in total order (80) but ranks 4th in revenue. | Facilitate knowledge sharing between sales rep. For example, have Raka share negotiations and upselling techniques for big clients, while Sari shares best practices for customer acquisition. Review Raka's discount structure to understand why his profit margin is lower than Dimas, who generates similar profit (Rp53.88 Jt) from less revenue. |
